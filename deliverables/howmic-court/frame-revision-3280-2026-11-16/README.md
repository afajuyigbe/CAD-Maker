# Howmic Court — 3,280 mm frame revision

**Dimensional review draft. Not for manufacture until checked by the responsible designer/fabricator.**

- [Download revised DXFs, review PDF and check records](HOWMIC-3280-REVIEW.zip)
- [Open the six-page review PDF](HOWMIC-frame-revision-REVIEW.pdf)

## Confirmed scope

Only the two lower braced frames shown in elevations A–A and B–B are revised. The grillage, including B4 and B7, is excluded.

The wall outside dimension is 3,800 mm, with 230 mm walls and a 3,340 mm clear gap. The revised frame envelope is 3,280 mm, giving 30 mm clearance at each end. The supplied fabrication rails are physically 3,350 mm long, so their reduction is 70 mm.

The existing 50 mm frame-edge to outer-post-centre offsets are retained:

| Frame | Revised dimension chain (mm) |
|---|---|
| A–A, single door | 50 + 1,246 + **681** + 1,253 + 50 = **3,280** |
| B–B, two doors | 50 + **690** + 1,805 + **685** + 50 = **3,280** |

The preserved marked door dimensions are between post centrelines, as drawn; they are not face-to-face clear openings. Confirm actual door requirements before manufacture.

## Package contents

- Twelve revised member/plate DXFs: B11–B14, BR3–BR7, p9-L, p9-R and p11.
- Unchanged C5–C12 and p10 profiles, copied from the supplied files.
- A coordinated 1:1 frame-elevation DXF, a six-page review PDF, member schedule, check records and file hashes.
- p9-L is quantity 2 and serves the 1,246 mm bay; p9-R is quantity 2 and serves the 1,253 mm bay. p11 remains quantity 4.

Both assembled envelopes were checked at 3,280 × 1,200 mm. Seventy mating hole centres agree within 0.010 mm, consistent with rounding in the source profiles. Plate outlines and minimum hole-edge ligaments are retained. These are geometric checks, not structural certification.

Review revised connections, bracing, anchorage, rod reach, packing across the 30 mm gaps, site measurements and clear door sizes before manufacture. The original Rev 0 DWGs and PDF are not amended or reissued by this package.
